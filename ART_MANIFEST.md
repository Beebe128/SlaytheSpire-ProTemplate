# Art Manifest — The Paintress Mod

This document tracks the source and status of all art assets in the mod.

## Status Legend
- **PLACEHOLDER**: Template asset reused; needs custom art
- **CUSTOM**: Original art created for this mod

## Card Art

All card art currently uses the **CardArtRoller** fallback system which automatically assigns recolored base game card art. Status: PLACEHOLDER for all 80 cards.

Cards pending custom art:
- All 80 cards in `/paintressResources/images/cards/`
